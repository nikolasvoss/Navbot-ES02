SLAM starts with the LiDAR service. The saved map loads on every start, which assumes the robot starts where that map began. Mapping runs slam_toolbox on LiDAR scans with the wheel odometry from the NavBot link bridge; without that service no scans are processed. Map and pose come from ROS topics /map and TF map → lds08rr. Angle zero and rotation direction of the LiDAR are uncalibrated, so the map may be mirrored or rotated.
