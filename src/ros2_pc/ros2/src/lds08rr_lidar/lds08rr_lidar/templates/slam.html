With a saved map, the robot localizes on it (Nav2 AMCL on the saved occupancy map) and the saved map does not change. Without one, or after switching to mapping, it maps with slam_toolbox. Mapping runs slam_toolbox on LiDAR scans with the wheel odometry from the NavBot link bridge; without that service no scans are processed. Map and pose come from ROS topics /map and TF map → base_link (the robot, not the sensor). Angle zero and rotation direction of the LiDAR are uncalibrated, so the map may be mirrored or rotated.
